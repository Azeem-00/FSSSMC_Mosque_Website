Who we are

Built around faith, service, and belonging.

The Federal Site & Services Scheme Muslim Community (FSSSMC) is a faith-centered space where believers gather to worship, learn, and serve with sincerity. Our mission is to create a warm and welcoming environment for worship, community care, and spiritual development.

Whether you are seeking guidance, looking to join a supportive community, or helping to organize outreach initiatives, FSSSMC is here to support your journey with compassion and purpose.
